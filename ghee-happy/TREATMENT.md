# "A Spoonful of Gold" — Crayon Picture Book, 60 s

**Format:** 1920×1080, 24 fps, 60.0 s, −14 LUFS. English narration + printed-word subtitles.
**Brand:** generic desi ghee (no real brand, logo or claims). Jar label reads only "GHEE".

## 1. Three structures considered

| | Structure | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A | A day of happiness: kitchen → meal → fridge | Expected; no medium-only moment |
| B | Cow → churn → pot → plate, four page turns | Origin story; reads as a process chart |
| **C** | **A grey page hides white-wax joy; one spoon of gold and an amber wash reveal it** | **Chosen: the happiness is a visual payoff only crayon can do** |

## 2. Logline and arc
A quiet girl faces a pale plate of dal and rice; her grandmother adds one spoonful of ghee, and a golden wash sweeps the page, revealing the happiness that was drawn in white wax from the very first frame.

- **Setup (0–15 s):** the page draws itself; everything is cool grey-brown line and bare paper; the girl is flat-mouthed.
- **Turn (15–25 s):** silence, then the spoon of gold lands.
- **Payoff (25–40 s):** the amber wash reveals white-wax hearts, smiling grains, flowers, sun; cheeks go pink.
- **Ending (40–60 s):** the family is coloured in around the table; pull back to the page on a fridge; page turn to the jar and the line.

Bookend: opens on a crayon tip in extreme close-up drawing a plain plate; ends with the plate drawn again, now golden, as the jar's lid.

## 3. Benchmark
- **Learn:** hand-textured frames that breathe (boil), wordless tenderness, one tune carrying a passage, still backgrounds with living figures (the Briggs picture-book grammar from STYLE.md); the Indian "ghee on hot roti" ad trope of *warmth as light*.
- **Do not take:** any character, song or image from existing books or ghee ads; no brand marks, no cow-and-farm cliché, no slogans from real brands.

## 4. Look
Warm paper `#f4efe3`. Crayon box (8): brown-plum line `#4a2f3a`, ghee gold `#e8a317`, butter `#f6d86b`, cream `#fff3d6`, tomato `#d9453a`, leaf `#6ba04a`, sky teal `#4aa3a8`, cheek pink `#f08a9a`, + white wax. Before the turn the page uses **only line + muddy grey-brown hatch** (a desaturated 3-colour subset); that is the "sad" palette. One watercolour: **amber**, which is the whole reveal. Characters are composited after the wash.

White-wax payoff hidden from frame one: hearts in the steam, smiles on rice grains, flowers on the tablecloth, a sun on the wall, sparkles around the girl. Present in the shot at 0–15 s but invisible.

## 6. Beat sheet (96 bpm → beat 0.625 s, bar 2.5 s)
- 0–2.5 bar 1: crayon scratch (no music); first stroke.
- 2.5–5: plate completes, pull-out; narration 1 at 2.2 s *"This is a plate."*
- 5–12.5 bars 3–5: ukulele alone, sparse; narration 2 at 6.5 *"It was a very quiet dinner."* Girl pokes rice twice (toy piano ticks).
- 12.5–15: push; clarinet sustained note.
- **15–17.5: silence** (room tone only, a distant clock). Jar clink.
- 17.5: **first glockenspiel note** = the spoon's tip touching rice. Narration 3 at 18 *"Then, one spoonful of gold."*
- 25–40: full theme (ukulele, music box, glock, recorder, shaker); wash sweeps on bars 11–14; a glock note per uncovered sparkle. Narration 4 at 28 *"And everything remembered how to smile."*
- 40–52: theme repeats, clarinet bass, family claps (woodblock). Narration 5 at 43 *"Warm. Golden. Home."*
- 50–54: music thins to ukulele + music box; page-turn foley at 54.2.
- 54.5–60: tagline lettered letter by letter with soft crayon foley; final chord on ukulele and glock at 58.75; ring-out to 60.

Two true silences (15–17.5 s; 53.8–54.2 s before the page turn). Two J/L-cuts: the amber wash swish begins 0.4 s before the wash is visible; the page-turn flap leads into the jar page.

## 8. Subtitles and titles
Printed words in Patrick Hand 52 px in the line colour on a ragged knock-out patch, bottom-centre, written left→right in 0.35 s, held ≥ 1.8 s and ≥ speech + 0.6 s. End line hand-lettered in Gaegu Bold: **"A spoonful of happy."** (written letter by letter; leaves by the camera/page, never a fade).

## 9. Known risks
- Software WebGL (SwiftShader) is slow: render at 1920×1080 with several workers; boil on twos and still backgrounds keep frames cheap.
- Wash text/wax rules from STYLE.md §9: place all type away from the wash; characters composite after the wash.
